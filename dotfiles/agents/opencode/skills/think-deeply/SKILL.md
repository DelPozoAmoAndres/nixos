## What I do
- Force the agent to think step by step before answering
- Validate every decision against requirements
- Consider edge cases, alternatives, and potential problems
- Produce only after thorough analysis

## When to use me
Use this skill when:
- You need high-quality, well-thought-out output
- The task is complex or has multiple valid approaches
- You want to avoid rushing to a solution
- Quality matters more than speed

## How to apply

### Before ANY action, do this:
1. **Restate the goal**: What exactly am I being asked to do?
2. **List constraints**: What are the limitations, requirements, and context?
3. **Consider 3 approaches**: What are the possible ways to solve this?
4. **Pick the best**: Which approach is most robust and why?
5. **Validate**: Does my chosen approach actually solve the problem?

### During execution:
- After each significant step, pause and ask: "Is this still aligned with the goal?"
- If something feels wrong, stop and re-evaluate
- Don't rush to finish — quality over speed

### Before delivering:
1. **Review the output**: Does it meet ALL requirements?
2. **Check for edge cases**: What could go wrong?
3. **Verify completeness**: Is anything missing?
4. **Self-critique**: What would a senior developer/designer say about this?

## Rules
- NEVER skip the thinking phase
- NEVER deliver without validation
- If uncertain, say so and explain why
- Prefer thoroughness over speed
